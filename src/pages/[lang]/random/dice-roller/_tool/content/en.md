## How to use

Choose how many dice and which type, then press "Roll" to see each die and the total. Switch to "Coin" to flip a coin.

Dice types: the standard six-sided d6 plus the polyhedral dice used in tabletop RPGs — d4, d8, d10, d12, d20 and the percentile d100.

## Is it fair?

Results come from your browser's cryptographically secure random number generator with a uniform distribution — every face has the same chance, like a perfect die.

## FAQ

**What are the odds of rolling 7 with two dice?** 6 in 36, or 1 in 6 — the most likely total. 2 and 12 are the least likely, 1 in 36 each.

**Does the last roll affect the next one?** No — every roll is independent. After five heads in a row, the chance of tails is still 50%.

For any range of numbers, use the [random number generator](/en/math/random-number/).
