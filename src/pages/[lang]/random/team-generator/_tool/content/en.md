## How to use

Paste the participants, one per line, choose the number of teams and press "Make teams". If people don't divide evenly, team sizes differ by at most one. Press again to reshuffle.

## FAQ

**How random is it?** The list is shuffled with your browser's cryptographically secure generator (Fisher–Yates), so every possible split is equally likely.

**Can I balance teams by skill?** Not automatically — the split is purely random. For more balanced teams, split your strongest players first and then everyone else, as two separate lists.

**Is my list saved?** No, it stays on the page and is never sent anywhere.

To pick a single winner, use the [random name picker](/en/random/random-picker/).
