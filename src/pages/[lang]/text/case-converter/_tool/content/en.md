## How to use

Paste your text and pick a style — the result appears instantly and can be copied with one click. Typed a paragraph with Caps Lock on? Choose "Sentence case".

## Styles

- **UPPERCASE** and **lowercase** — the whole text in one case.
- **Sentence case** — a capital letter only at the start of each sentence.
- **Title Case** — every word capitalized, for headings and names.
- **camelCase**, **PascalCase**, **snake_case**, **kebab-case** — for variable names, file names and URLs in code: `myVariableName`, `my_variable_name`, `my-variable-name`.

## FAQ

**Does Title Case follow AP or Chicago style?** No — every word is capitalized, including short words like "a", "of" and "the". Lowercase them by hand if your style guide requires it.

**Are punctuation and line breaks kept?** In the regular styles, yes — only letters change. In camelCase, snake_case and kebab-case punctuation is removed and only the words remain.

**Does it work with accented letters?** Yes, case conversion works for any alphabet.

To count words and characters, use the [word counter](/en/text/word-counter/).
