## How to use

Paste a list — one item per line — and choose an action. Copy the result back into a spreadsheet, a document or your code.

## What you can do

- **Remove duplicates** — keeps the first occurrence of each line and the original order.
- **Duplicates, ignore case** — "London" and "london" count as the same; surrounding spaces don't matter.
- **Sort A→Z and Z→A** — natural sorting: numbers compare by value, so 2 comes before 10.
- **Remove empty lines** — including lines with only spaces.
- **Trim spaces** — at the start and end of every line.
- **Reverse order** — flips the list.

## FAQ

**How do I remove duplicates from an Excel or Google Sheets column?** Copy the column, paste it here, choose "Remove duplicates" and paste the result back.

**Is there a size limit?** No — everything runs in your browser; lists with tens of thousands of lines are fine.

To count words and characters, use the [word counter](/en/text/word-counter/).
