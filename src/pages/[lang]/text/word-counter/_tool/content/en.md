## How to use

Paste or type your text into the box — the counts update as you type. Your text never leaves your device: everything is counted right in the browser.

## What gets counted

- **Words** — runs of letters and digits. Contractions like "don't" and hyphenated words like "well-known" count as one word.
- **Characters** — every character, including spaces and line breaks. Emoji and accented letters count as one character, the way you see them.
- **Characters without spaces** — the same, minus spaces, tabs and line breaks.
- **Sentences** — split by sentence-ending punctuation using the rules of the language.
- **Paragraphs** — non-empty lines.

## When it's useful

- Checking an essay, article or application against a word limit.
- Staying within limits: a page's meta description is about 155 characters, a post on X is 280.
- Estimating translation or copywriting fees charged per word.

## FAQ

**Why does my word processor show a different number?** Editors treat hyphenated words, numbers and punctuation slightly differently.

**Is there a length limit?** No, only your device's memory.
