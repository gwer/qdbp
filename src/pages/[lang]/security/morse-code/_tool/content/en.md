## How to use

To translate text to Morse code, choose "To Morse" and type — letters, numbers and punctuation are supported. To decode, choose "From Morse" and paste the dots and dashes.

Letters are separated by a space and words by a slash `/`. You can also paste `·` and `—` instead of dots and dashes.

## Examples

- SOS → `... --- ...`
- Hello world → `.... . .-.. .-.. --- / .-- --- .-. .-.. -..`

## FAQ

**Why is SOS used as the distress signal?** Not because it stands for anything — three dots, three dashes, three dots is simply easy to send and hard to mistake.

**What's the timing in real Morse?** A dash is three dots long; letters are separated by a gap of three dots and words by seven.

**Is Morse code a cipher?** No — it's a public code with no key. For simple encryption, try the [Caesar cipher](/en/security/caesar-cipher/).
