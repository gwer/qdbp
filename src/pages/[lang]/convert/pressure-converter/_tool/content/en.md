## How to use

Enter a pressure and pick the units — the result and a table in every pressure unit appear instantly.

## Definitions

- 1 psi (pound per square inch) ≈ **6.895 kPa**.
- 1 bar = **100 kPa** ≈ 14.5 psi.
- 1 standard atmosphere = **101.325 kPa** = 14.696 psi = 760 mmHg.
- 1 inch of mercury (inHg) ≈ 3.386 kPa — used in US weather reports.

## Examples

- 32 psi (a common tire pressure) = **2.21 bar** = 220.6 kPa.
- 35 psi = **2.41 bar**.
- 2.2 bar (a European tire spec) = **31.9 psi**.

## FAQ

**Is bar the same as atmosphere?** Close: 1 bar = 0.987 atm. For tire pressure the 1–2% difference doesn't matter, but the table shows exact values.

**What's normal atmospheric pressure?** 29.92 inHg = 1013.25 hPa (millibars) = 14.7 psi.
