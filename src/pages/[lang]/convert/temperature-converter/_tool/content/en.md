## How to use

Enter a temperature and pick the scales — the result appears instantly. Use a minus sign for negative temperatures.

## Formulas

- °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
- °F = °C × 9/5 + 32
- K = °C + 273.15

## Examples

- 20 °C = **68 °F** — a comfortable room temperature.
- 180 °C (a common oven setting in European recipes) = **356 °F**, in practice 350–360 °F.
- 37 °C = **98.6 °F** — normal body temperature.
- −40 °C = −40 °F — the only point where the scales meet.

## FAQ

**Quick mental conversion from Celsius to Fahrenheit?** Double it and add 30: 25 °C → about 80 °F (exactly 77 °F). For ovens, the [oven temperature converter](/en/kitchen/oven-temperature/) also covers gas marks.

**What's absolute zero?** 0 K = −273.15 °C = −459.67 °F — nothing can be colder.
