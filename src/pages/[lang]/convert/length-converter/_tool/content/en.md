## How to use

Enter a number, choose the units to convert from and to, and the result appears instantly. Below it you'll see the same value in every length unit. "Swap" reverses the direction.

## Definitions

- 1 inch = **2.54 cm** exactly (international agreement of 1959).
- 1 foot = 12 inches = **30.48 cm**.
- 1 yard = 3 feet = **0.9144 m**.
- 1 mile = 1,760 yards = **1.609344 km**.
- 1 nautical mile = **1,852 m**, used in aviation and at sea.

## Examples

- 6 ft = **1.8288 m**.
- 5 ft 10 in (70 in) = **177.8 cm**.
- A 10K race is **6.21 miles**, a marathon (42.195 km) **26.22 miles**.

## FAQ

**How many centimeters are in an inch?** Exactly 2.54.

**How do I convert feet and inches to centimeters?** Multiply feet by 12, add the inches, then multiply by 2.54. For height-based calculations, the [BMI calculator](/en/health/bmi/) accepts entries like 5'10" directly.

For areas, use the [area converter](/en/convert/area-converter/).
