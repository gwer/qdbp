## How to use

Enter a number and pick the units — the result appears instantly, with a table of the value in every weight unit below.

## Definitions

- 1 pound = **0.45359237 kg** exactly; 1 kg ≈ 2.2046 lb.
- 1 ounce = 1/16 lb = **28.349523125 g**.
- 1 stone = 14 lb ≈ **6.35 kg** — common for body weight in the UK.
- A US (short) ton = 2,000 lb ≈ 907.18 kg; a UK (long) ton = 2,240 lb ≈ 1,016.05 kg; a metric ton = 1,000 kg.
- 1 carat = **0.2 g**, for gemstones.

## Examples

- 150 lb = **68.04 kg**.
- 70 kg = **154.32 lb**.
- 100 g = **3.53 oz**.

## FAQ

**How many kilograms are in a pound?** 0.45359237 kg — about 454 g. Quick estimate: divide pounds by 2.2.

**Is this weight or mass?** Strictly speaking, kilograms and pounds measure mass; in everyday language we call it weight.
