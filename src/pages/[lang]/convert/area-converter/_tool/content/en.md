## How to use

Enter an area and pick the units — the result and a table in every area unit appear instantly.

## Definitions

- 1 square foot = **0.09290304 m²**; 1 m² ≈ 10.76 sq ft.
- 1 acre = 43,560 sq ft ≈ **4,046.86 m²** ≈ 0.405 hectares.
- 1 square mile = 640 acres.
- 1 hectare = 10,000 m² ≈ 2.47 acres.

## Examples

- 1,000 sq ft = **92.9 m²**.
- 1 hectare = **2.47 acres**.
- 50 m² = **538.2 sq ft**.

## FAQ

**How many square feet are in an acre?** 43,560.

**How do I find the square footage of a room?** Multiply length by width in feet. For flooring, the [laminate calculator](/en/repair/laminate/) adds waste and box counts.

For lengths, use the [length converter](/en/convert/length-converter/).
