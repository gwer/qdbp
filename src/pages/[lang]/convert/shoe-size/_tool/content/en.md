## How to use

Pick a system and enter a size — you'll see it in the other systems along with foot length. If you know your foot length, choose "Foot length, cm": it's rounded up to the next 5 mm so the shoe isn't shorter than your foot.

## How the systems work

- **US and UK** sizes step by a third of an inch (8.47 mm) but start from different points. US men's is one size above UK, and US women's is one size above US men's.
- **European (EU)** sizes count the last length in Paris points of 2/3 cm.
- **Foot length in millimeters** (Mondopoint, ISO 9407) is the most precise.

The chart follows the foot-length conversion in Wikipedia's "Shoe size" article. Lasts vary between brands, so half-size differences are normal — check the maker's own chart before buying.

## Examples

- US men's 10 = **EU 42.5** = UK 9, foot 27 cm (10.6 in).
- US women's 7.5 = **EU 38**.
- A 24.3 cm foot = **US women's 8**, EU 38.5.

## FAQ

**How do I measure my foot?** Stand on paper, mark your heel and longest toe, and measure between the marks. Measure both feet in the evening and use the larger one.

**What about kids' sizes?** Not included — children's scales vary too much between countries and brands for one reliable chart.
