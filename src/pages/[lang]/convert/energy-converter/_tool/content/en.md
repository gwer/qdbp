## How to use

Enter a value and pick the units — the result and a table in every energy unit appear instantly.

## Definitions

- 1 kWh = **3,412.14 BTU** = 3.6 MJ ≈ 860 kcal — electricity is billed in kWh.
- 1 BTU (British thermal unit) ≈ **1,055 J** — used for heating and AC capacity.
- 1 food Calorie = 1 kilocalorie = **4.184 kJ**.

## Examples

- 1 kWh = **3,412 BTU**.
- A 12,000 BTU/h air conditioner ≈ **3.5 kW** of cooling.
- 2,000 kcal = **8,368 kJ**.

## FAQ

**Is a food Calorie the same as a calorie?** No — a food Calorie (capital C) is a kilocalorie, 1,000 small calories.

**How do I convert BTU/h to watts?** The ratio is the same as BTU → Wh: 1 BTU/h ≈ 0.293 W.
