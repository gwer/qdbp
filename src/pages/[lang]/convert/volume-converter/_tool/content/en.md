## How to use

Enter a number and pick the units — the result and a table in every volume unit appear instantly.

## Definitions

- A US gallon = **3.785411784 L**; a UK (imperial) gallon = **4.54609 L** — they're different, so watch out with recipes and fuel economy.
- 1 US gallon = 4 quarts = 8 pints = 16 cups = 128 fl oz.
- 1 US cup ≈ **236.6 mL**, 1 tablespoon ≈ 14.8 mL, 1 teaspoon ≈ 4.9 mL.
- A UK pint = 568.26 mL (vs. 473.18 mL for a US pint); a UK fl oz ≈ 28.41 mL.
- 1 liter = 1,000 mL; 1 cubic meter = **1,000 L**.

## Examples

- 1 L = **4.23 US cups**.
- 1 US gallon = **3.79 L**.
- 500 mL = **16.91 US fl oz**.

## FAQ

**Why are there two gallons?** The imperial gallon was defined in 1824 as the volume of 10 lb of water; the US gallon comes from the older wine gallon.

**How do I convert cups of flour to grams?** That depends on the ingredient — use the [cups to grams converter](/en/kitchen/cups-to-grams/).
