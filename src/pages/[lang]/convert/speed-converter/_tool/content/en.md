## How to use

Enter a speed and pick the units — the result and a table in every speed unit appear instantly.

## Definitions

- 1 mph = **1.609344 km/h**; 1 km/h ≈ 0.6214 mph.
- 1 m/s = **3.6 km/h** ≈ 2.237 mph.
- 1 knot = 1 nautical mile per hour = **1.852 km/h** ≈ 1.151 mph.

## Examples

- 60 mph = **96.56 km/h**.
- 100 km/h (a common speed limit abroad) = **62.14 mph**; 130 km/h = **80.78 mph**.
- A 10 m/s wind = **36 km/h** ≈ 22.4 mph.

## FAQ

**How do I convert km/h to mph in my head?** Multiply by 0.6 (or by 5 and divide by 8): 100 km/h → about 62 mph.

**What's a knot?** One nautical mile (1,852 m) per hour — used for wind, boats and aircraft.

For running speeds, the [running pace calculator](/en/health/running-pace/) shows pace per mile.
