## How to use

Enter a fuel economy figure and pick the units — the result and a table in every unit appear instantly.

## Formulas

- L/100 km = 235.215 / mpg (US).
- L/100 km = 282.481 / mpg (UK).
- km/L = 100 / (L/100 km).

Higher mpg or km/L means a more efficient car; with L/100 km, lower is better.

## Examples

- 30 mpg (US) = **7.84 L/100 km**.
- 8 L/100 km = **29.4 mpg** (US) = 35.3 mpg (UK).
- 6 L/100 km = **39.2 mpg** (US).

## FAQ

**Why are UK mpg figures higher than US ones?** The imperial gallon is bigger (4.546 L vs 3.785 L), so a car goes further on it.

**How do I calculate my car's real mpg?** Fill up, reset the trip meter, drive, fill up again and divide miles driven by the gallons added.
