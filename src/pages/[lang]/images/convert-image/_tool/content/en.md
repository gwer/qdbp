## How to use

Pick the format you need and add your files — drag them in, choose them or paste from the clipboard. The results are ready instantly: download them one by one or as a ZIP. Image dimensions stay the same.

Conversion runs in your browser; files are never uploaded.

## Which format should I choose?

- **JPG** — for photos: small files that open everywhere. No transparency, so transparent areas are filled with the background color you choose (white by default).
- **PNG** — for screenshots, diagrams, logos and images with transparency. Lossless, but photos saved as PNG are large.
- **WebP** — a modern format for the web: usually lighter than JPG and PNG at comparable quality, with transparency support. Works in all modern browsers, but not in some older apps.

## FAQ

**How do I convert WebP to JPG?** Add the .webp file and choose "JPG" — handy when a website or app doesn't accept WebP.

**Can I convert HEIC photos from an iPhone?** Only in browsers that can open HEIC themselves, such as Safari. Elsewhere the file won't open — use the [HEIC to JPG converter](/en/images/heic-to-jpg/), which works in any browser.

**Why isn't AVIF available?** The AVIF option only appears in browsers that can save in that format. Most can't yet.

**Will quality drop?** Saving as JPG or WebP loses a little, depending on the Quality slider — 85–90% is hard to tell apart by eye. PNG is lossless.

To make images smaller in pixels, use the [image resizer](/en/images/resize-image/); to reduce file size, the [image compressor](/en/images/compress-image/).
