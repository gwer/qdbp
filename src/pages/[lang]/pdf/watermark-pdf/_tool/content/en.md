## How to use

Choose a PDF and enter the watermark text — the preview on the right shows how it will sit on the page. Set the size, color and opacity, then pick the angle and layout:

- **Once, centered** — large text in the middle of the page, the classic “SAMPLE”.
- **Across the page** — the text repeats in rows. It's harder to cut out or paint over, which is why copies of documents sent to strangers are protected this way.

Click “Add” and download the file. By default the watermark goes on all pages, but you can enter specific ones: `1`, `2-5`.

## Why watermark copies of documents

When you need to send scans of your passport, driver's license or a contract to a landlord, an employer or a service, it's useful to state on the copy who it's for and why. For example: “Copy for Acme Inc., rental application, 11/06/2026”. Such a copy is harder to use for anything else. Put the text over the data, but keep the data readable: 20–35% opacity is usually enough.

## How it works

The text is placed on top of the page content; the document itself isn't re-saved. Rotated pages are taken into account: the watermark sits the way the page appears on screen.

Latin text is set in Helvetica. Cyrillic, Greek and other scripts are embedded as high-resolution images, so any language works.

The file is processed in your browser and never uploaded.

## FAQ

**Can the watermark be removed later?** In a PDF editor, yes: the watermark is a separate object on top of the page. To guard against simple removal, use the “Across the page” layout. It's even more reliable to then [compress the PDF](/en/pdf/compress-pdf/) with the “As a scan” method: the pages become images, and the watermark becomes part of the image. The file usually gets larger.

**How do I also prevent copying or printing?** Set a password and restrictions with the [PDF password protector](/en/pdf/protect-pdf/).
