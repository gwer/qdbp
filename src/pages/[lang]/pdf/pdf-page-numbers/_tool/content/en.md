## How to use

Choose a PDF and set up the numbers — the preview on the right shows how the page will look:

- **Position** — click one of the six spots on the page diagram: top or bottom, left, center or right.
- **Format** — just the number, “3 / 10”, “– 3 –”, “Page 3” or “Page 3 of 10”.
- **Start at** — the number to start counting from.
- **Pages to number** — an empty field means all pages. For example, `2-` leaves the cover page without a number.
- **Font size** and **distance from edge** — 10–12 pt and 10–15 mm suit most documents.

Click “Number” and download the file.

## Examples

- Cover page without a number, numbering starts on the second page as “2”: pages `2-`, start at `2`.
- Cover page without a number, the second page counts as the first: pages `2-`, start at `1`.
- The second part of a document continues the numbering of the first part, which had 48 pages: start at `49`.

## How it works

The number is placed on top of the page content; the document itself isn't re-saved, so text, images, bookmarks and forms stay as they were. Rotated pages are taken into account: the number goes where you see it and reads upright.

Digits and Latin text are set in Helvetica, a font every PDF program has. Labels in other scripts, such as Cyrillic or Greek, are embedded as high-resolution images: they print just as sharply, but can't be selected as text.

The “of N” number is the last page number: starting at 1 with ten numbered pages gives “of 10”.

The file is processed in your browser and never uploaded.

## FAQ

**The number overlaps text at the bottom of the page. What can I do?** Increase the distance from the edge, move the number to the top or make the font smaller.

**Can I remove page numbers that are already in the file?** No: they're part of the page content, like any other text.

**How do I number several files continuously?** First [merge them](/en/pdf/merge-pdf/) into one file, then add the numbers.
