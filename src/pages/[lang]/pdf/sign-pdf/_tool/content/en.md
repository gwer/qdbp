## How to use

1. Choose the PDF you need to sign.
2. Create a signature in one of three ways:
   - **draw** it with your finger on a phone or with a mouse on a computer;
   - **type** your name and pick a style, from handwritten to plain italic;
   - **upload a photo** of your signature on white paper: the background becomes transparent and the extra margins are cropped.
3. Click “Add signature” and it appears on the open page. Drag it into place and adjust the size with the blue dot in the corner. The “Add date” button adds today's date the same way, as a separate block. Any other text works too — for example, the printed name or initials many forms ask for next to the signature: type it in the “Text” field and click “Add text”.
4. Flip through the pages with the arrows and sign wherever needed — you can place the same signature as many times as you like.
5. Click “Save PDF” and download the signed file.

## How it works

The signature is saved as an image with a transparent background and placed on top of the page. The document itself isn't re-saved: text, form fields and the rest of the content stay as they were. Drawing, photo processing and saving all happen in your browser: neither the document nor the signature is sent to a server or stored anywhere.

## Is this a digital signature?

No. It's an image of a signature — the same as printing a document, signing it with a pen and scanning it. That's enough when you're expected to send a signed scan: an application, a consent form, a rental application, an agreement with a person or a company that accepts scans. Many organizations accept signed scans.

A certificate-based digital signature is something else: a cryptographic signature tied to a certificate issued to the signer. It's created with special software and a key — for example, a certificate-based signature in Acrobat or a qualified electronic signature under eIDAS in the EU — and an image can't replace it. When a certificate-based digital signature is required, this tool doesn't create one.

## FAQ

**My signature has a white rectangle around it. What can I do?** When uploading a photo, check that “Remove white background” is on (it is by default), and photograph your signature on clean white paper in even light, without shadows: a grey shadow doesn't count as white.

**Can I sign several files at once?** Sign each one in turn, or first [merge them](/en/pdf/merge-pdf/) into one document.

**How do I make the signature impossible to remove?** After signing, [compress the PDF](/en/pdf/compress-pdf/) with the “As a scan” method: the pages become images together with the signature. Text can no longer be selected after that, and the file usually gets larger.
