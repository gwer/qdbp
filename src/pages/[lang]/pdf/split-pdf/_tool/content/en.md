## How to use

Choose a PDF and thumbnails of all its pages appear. Then pick a method:

- **Every N pages** — the file is cut into equal parts. With N = 1 every page becomes a separate PDF, with N = 2 you get two pages per file, and so on. The last part may be shorter.
- **By ranges** — list the parts separated by commas: `1-3, 4-10, 11-` gives three files. Ranges can overlap, and `5-1` puts the pages in reverse order.
- **Select pages** — select the pages you need and get them as one file or each one separately. This is handy for pulling a few pages out of a document, such as a scan of your ID from a set of paperwork.

The number in the corner of each thumbnail shows which file the page goes to; faded pages don't go anywhere. Click “Split” and download the parts one by one or all at once as a ZIP.

## How it works

Pages are copied into new files as they are, without re-compression: text stays text, and scans don't lose quality. Fonts and images shared by several pages go into every part that needs them, so the parts together can be larger than the original file.

The file is never uploaded: splitting happens in your browser. If the PDF has a password to open it, you're asked for it right after choosing the file. The parts are saved without a password.

## FAQ

**How do I delete unwanted pages from a PDF?** You can select the pages you need here and save them as one file. It's easier in the [PDF page organizer](/en/pdf/organize-pdf/): there you delete pages with a button, and you can reorder and rotate the rest.

**Why are bookmarks and links to other pages gone?** The parts are built from pages anew, while the table of contents and internal links describe the original document as a whole. Links to websites are kept.

**How do I put the parts back together?** In the [PDF merger](/en/pdf/merge-pdf/), in any order.
