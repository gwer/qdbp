## How to use

Choose a PDF and thumbnails of its pages appear. There are two ways to rotate them:

- **the whole document** — with the “All left” or “All right” buttons above the thumbnails;
- **a single page** — with the circular arrows under its thumbnail.

Each click rotates a page 90°, two clicks give 180° — that's how you fix scans that are upside down. The thumbnails show the result right away. Click “Save PDF” and download the file.

## How it works

Every PDF page has a rotation property — an angle that's a multiple of 90°. The tool changes only that; the content isn't re-saved. So the quality of text and scans doesn't suffer, and the file size barely changes. Bookmarks, form fields and document properties are kept.

The rotation is saved in the file itself, so the page is rotated in any program: in a browser, in Acrobat, on a phone and when printing.

The file is processed in your browser and never uploaded. If the PDF has a password to open it, enter the password first. The saved file won't have a password.

## FAQ

**The page is rotated in my viewer but not when I print. Why?** Most likely the page was rotated only in the program's window, without saving it to the file. Here the rotation is written into the PDF itself.

**Can I rotate by any angle, say 5°, to straighten a crooked scan?** No. The PDF format rotates pages only by multiples of 90°. Straightening a scan means redrawing the image, and this tool doesn't do that.

**How do I also delete or reorder pages?** The [PDF page organizer](/en/pdf/organize-pdf/) does all of that in one place, along with rotation.
