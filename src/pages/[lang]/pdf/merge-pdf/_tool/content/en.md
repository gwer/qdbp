## How to use

Add two or more PDFs: drag them into the box or choose them on your device. You can add files in several rounds. The list shows them in the order they'll appear in the final document. Change the order by dragging or with the arrows on the right; remove a file with the cross.

To take only some pages of a file, enter them in the “Pages” field:

- `1-3` — pages one to three;
- `1, 4, 7` — individual pages;
- `5-` — from page five to the end;
- `3-1` — the first three pages in reverse order.

An empty field means the whole file. Click “Merge” and download the finished PDF.

If a file has a password to open it, enter the password right in its row. The merged file won't have that protection.

## How it works

Pages are copied into a new document without re-compression, so the quality of text, images and scans doesn't change. Fonts and images repeated across pages of the same file are copied only once.

Everything happens in your browser: files are never uploaded, so there's no queue and no daily limit. A single file can be up to 300 MB; the overall limit is your device's memory. Hundreds of pages and tens of megabytes usually go through fine, even on a phone.

## FAQ

**Are bookmarks and links kept?** No. Merging carries over the pages themselves — text, images, links to websites. The table of contents (bookmarks), fillable form fields and links to other pages of the document aren't carried over. Almost all PDF merging tools work this way.

**Can I add an image or a scan from my phone?** First [convert the images to PDF](/en/pdf/jpg-to-pdf/), then merge the resulting file with the rest.

**How do I reorder or delete pages after merging?** Open the finished file in the [PDF page organizer](/en/pdf/organize-pdf/).

**The merged file is too large. What can I do?** The size usually comes from scans and photos. [Compress the PDF](/en/pdf/compress-pdf/): the images inside are re-saved at a lower resolution.
