## How to use

Choose a PDF and thumbnails of all its pages appear. Each thumbnail has buttons underneath:

- the trash can deletes the page. It stays in place, faded, and the same button brings it back;
- the circular arrows rotate the page 90° left or right;
- the angled arrows move the page one place back or forward.

Reordering is easier by dragging: grab a thumbnail and drop it in its new place. The “All left” and “All right” buttons rotate the whole document at once, for example when a scan came out sideways.

When you're done, click “Save PDF” and download the file. The original file doesn't change.

## How it works

Changes are made in the document itself, not in a copy. So everything not tied to the deleted pages is kept: the table of contents (bookmarks), fillable fields, document properties, attached files. Page content isn't re-compressed, and quality doesn't change.

Rotation is stored in the page's properties, the way Acrobat and other editors do it. The image isn't re-saved, so you can rotate a page as many times as you like without loss.

The file is processed in your browser and never uploaded. If the PDF has a password to open it, enter the password first. The saved file won't have a password.

## FAQ

**Why did the file barely shrink after I deleted lots of pages?** Fonts and images are often shared by the whole document and stay as long as at least one page uses them. To reduce the size noticeably, [compress the PDF](/en/pdf/compress-pdf/).

**How do I pull a few pages out into a separate file?** Use the [PDF splitter](/en/pdf/split-pdf/) with the “Select pages” mode.

**Can I add pages from another file?** First combine the files in the [PDF merger](/en/pdf/merge-pdf/), then arrange the pages here.
