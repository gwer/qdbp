## How to use

Enter an amount, the VAT rate and choose what to do:

- **Add VAT** — you have a net price and need the price including VAT;
- **Remove VAT** — you have a price including VAT and need to know how much of it is tax.

The default rate is **20%**, the standard VAT rate in the UK. Reduced rates (5%) and zero-rated goods also exist, and other countries have their own rates — just type the one you need.

## Formulas

- Add: `VAT = net × rate / 100`
- Remove: `VAT = gross × rate / (100 + rate)`

## Examples

- £250 net + 20% VAT = **£300**, of which £50 is VAT.
- Remove 20% VAT from £120: VAT is **£20** (one sixth of the gross amount), net £100.
- US sales tax works like "Add": $100 at 8.25% = $108.25.

## FAQ

**Why can't I just take 20% of the gross price?** Because VAT is charged on the net price. 20% of £120 is £24 — wrong; the VAT included in £120 is £20.

**Does it work for EU VAT, GST or sales tax?** Yes, the arithmetic is the same — enter the rate that applies.

This is a quick estimate, not tax advice.
