## How to use

Enter the original price and the discount percentage — you'll see the sale price and how much you save. Use the “Find” field for the reverse questions: the original price from a sale price, or the discount percentage from the old and new prices.

## Formula

`sale price = price × (1 − discount / 100)`

For example, $59.99 at 30% off: 59.99 × 0.7 = **$41.99**, you save **$18.00**.

## Examples

- A $120 jacket at 25% off costs $90 — you save $30.
- Two discounts in a row, 20% and then another 10%, are not 30% off: $100 → $80 → **$72**, which is 28% off in total.

## FAQ

**How do I find the original price?** Divide the sale price by (1 − discount / 100): $90 / 0.75 = $120.

**How do I work out the discount percentage?** Divide the amount saved by the original price and multiply by 100: 30 / 120 × 100 = 25%.

**Is sales tax included?** No. In the US, sales tax is usually added at checkout on the discounted price.

**Do a discount and a markup of the same percentage cancel out?** No: $100 − 20% = $80, but $80 + 20% = $96. To price products, use the [markup calculator](/en/finance/markup/).
