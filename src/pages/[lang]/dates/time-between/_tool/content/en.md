## How to use

Enter a start time and an end time — you'll see the hours and minutes between them, plus the duration in minutes and in decimal hours (handy for timesheets and pay).

If the end time is earlier than the start time, it's treated as the next day — that's how night shifts are counted.

## Examples

- 9:15 AM to 6:40 PM — **9 hours 25 minutes**, which is 565 minutes or 9.42 hours.
- 10:00 PM to 6:30 AM — **8 hours 30 minutes** across midnight, 8.5 hours.
- 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM — 9 hours; with an unpaid one-hour lunch, 8 paid hours.

## FAQ

**How do I convert minutes to decimal hours?** Divide the minutes by 60: 25 minutes is 0.42 hours, so 9:25 is 9.42 hours.

**Can it handle more than 24 hours?** This calculator works within a single day. For dates, use the [days calculator](/en/dates/days-between/); to add several days to a time, use the [time calculator](/en/dates/time-calculator/).

**Is daylight saving time taken into account?** No, it's the plain clock difference.
