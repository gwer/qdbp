## How to use

Enter a date of birth. By default the age is calculated as of today, but you can pick any other date — for example, to check how old someone will be on a school cutoff date or an application deadline.

## How age is calculated

First the calculator counts full months from the date of birth, then the remaining days. Short months are taken into account: from January 31 to February 29 in a leap year is 29 days, not "one month".

If someone was born on February 29, their birthday in non-leap years is counted as February 28.

## Example

Born on June 15, 1990, age on October 19, 2026:

- **36 years, 4 months and 4 days**;
- **13,275 days** in total;
- **239 days** until the next birthday.

## FAQ

**Why is the result one day less than I expected?** Age is counted in full days: you turn a year older on your birthday, not the day before.

**Can I calculate age on a past date?** Yes, pick it in the second field. It can't be earlier than the date of birth.

**Do time zones matter?** No, only calendar dates are used; the time of day is ignored.
