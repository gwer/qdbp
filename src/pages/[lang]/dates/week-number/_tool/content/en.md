## How to use

The current week number is shown by default. Pick any date to see its week number and the first and last day of that week.

## ISO 8601 vs. the US system

In the **US**, weeks start on Sunday and week 1 is the week that contains January 1.

Most of Europe and many international organizations use **ISO 8601**:

- weeks start on Monday;
- week 1 is the week that contains the first Thursday of January.

Because of this, the last days of December can belong to week 1 of the next year, and the first days of January to the last week of the previous year. For example, January 1, 2027 is in **week 53 of 2026** under ISO 8601. Week numbers in the two systems can differ by one.

## Example

October 19, 2026 is in **week 43** under both systems: Monday, October 19 to Sunday, October 25 in ISO 8601, and Sunday, October 18 to Saturday, October 24 in the US system.

## FAQ

**How many weeks are in a year?** Under ISO 8601, 52 or 53. 2026 has 53 ISO weeks.
