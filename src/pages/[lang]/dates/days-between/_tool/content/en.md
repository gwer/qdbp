## How to use

Pick a start date and an end date. You'll instantly see the number of days, weeks and weekdays (Monday to Friday) between them.

## Should the end date be included?

By default the calculator returns the difference between the dates: from the 1st to the 2nd is **1 day**. If you need every day of a period counted (a vacation, a hotel stay, a project duration), turn on "Include end date" — then the 1st to the 2nd counts as 2 days.

## Examples

- January 1 to December 31, 2026 — **364 days**; with the end date included, 365 days and **261 weekdays**.
- October 19, 2026 to New Year's Day — **74 days**.
- November 2 to November 30, 2026 without the end date — 28 days, **20 of them weekdays**.

## FAQ

**Are public holidays excluded?** No, weekdays are simply Monday through Friday. Holidays are not taken into account.

**What if the end date is before the start date?** The result is negative — that's how many days ago the date was.

**Are leap years handled?** Yes, February 29 counts as a normal day.
