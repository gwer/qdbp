## How to use

Enter a distance and a time to see your pace (minutes per mile or kilometer) and average speed, plus predicted times for standard race distances. Enter time as `h:mm:ss` or `mm:ss`, e.g. `25:00` or `1:52:00`.

Use “Find” for the other questions: your finish time at a given pace (say, a marathon at 8:00 per mile) or how far you'll go in a given time. For standard races — 5K, 10K, half marathon, marathon — just pick the race instead of typing the distance. The splits table shows the elapsed time at every mile (or kilometer) at an even pace.

## How it's calculated

- Pace = time / distance: a 5K in 25:00 is **8:03 per mile**.
- Speed = distance / time: that's about **7.5 mph**.
- Predictions use Peter Riegel's formula: `T₂ = T₁ × (D₂ / D₁)^1.06` — the longer the race, the more your pace slows.

## Example

5K in 25:00 → 8:03 min/mi. Predicted: 10K **52:07**, half marathon **1:55:00**, marathon **3:59:47**.

## FAQ

**How accurate is the marathon prediction?** It's a guide for runners who train for long distances. Without enough mileage, real marathon times are usually noticeably slower.

**What's a good running pace?** It depends on age, experience and goals. Easy runs are usually done at a conversational pace.

**Can I use kilometers?** Yes — switch units to metric to see pace per kilometer.
