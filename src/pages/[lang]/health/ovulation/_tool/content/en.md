## How to use

Enter the first day of your last period and your usual cycle length to see your estimated ovulation date, your fertile window — the days when you're most likely to conceive — and when your next period is due.

## How it's calculated

The second half of the cycle, from ovulation to your period, lasts about **14 days** for most women, so ovulation is expected 14 days before your next period:

`ovulation = first day of cycle + cycle length − 14`

Sperm can survive up to 5 days and an egg about a day. That gives the **fertile window**: the 5 days before ovulation, ovulation day and the day after.

Example: a 28-day cycle that started on March 1. Ovulation is around **March 15**, the fertile window runs March 10–16, and the next period is due March 29.

## FAQ

**How accurate is this?** It's an estimate. Ovulation can shift by several days even in a regular cycle because of stress, illness or travel. Ovulation tests, basal body temperature tracking and ultrasound monitoring give a more precise picture.

**Can I use this to avoid pregnancy?** No. The calendar method is one of the least reliable forms of birth control.

**What if my cycle is irregular?** Run the calculation with your shortest and your longest cycle from the past six months — your fertile window is likely somewhere in between.

If you're already pregnant, the [due date calculator](/en/health/due-date/) shows how far along you are.
