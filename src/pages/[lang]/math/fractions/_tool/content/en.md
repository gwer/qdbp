## How to use

Enter the numerators and denominators of two fractions and choose an operation. The result is simplified automatically and shown three ways: as a fraction, as a mixed number and as a decimal.

For a negative fraction, put a minus sign in front of the numerator. A whole number is a fraction with a denominator of 1.

## How it works

- Adding and subtracting: bring the fractions to a common denominator — `a/b ± c/d = (a·d ± c·b) / (b·d)`.
- Multiplying: `a/b × c/d = (a·c) / (b·d)`.
- Dividing: multiply by the flipped fraction — `a/b ÷ c/d = (a·d) / (b·c)`.
- Then divide the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor.

## Examples

- 1/2 + 1/3 = **5/6**.
- 2/3 − 1/4 = **5/12**.
- 3/4 ÷ 1/8 = **6**.
- 7/4 + 1/2 = **9/4**, which is **2 1/4** or 2.25.

## FAQ

**What is a mixed number?** An improper fraction written with its whole part: 9/4 = 2 1/4.

**Can I divide by zero?** No — if the second fraction is zero, division isn't possible.
