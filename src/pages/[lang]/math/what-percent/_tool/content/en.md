## How to use

Enter the part and the total — the calculator shows what percentage the part is of the total. The result updates as you type.

## Formula

`percentage = part × 100 / total`

For example, 30 out of 150: 30 × 100 / 150 = **20%**.

## Examples

- 45 correct answers out of 60 questions: 45 × 100 / 60 = **75%**.
- A score of 42 out of 50: **84%**.
- Sales of 250 against a target of 200: **125%** of target.
- 1 out of 3 is **33.33%** (exactly one third).

## FAQ

**Can the result be more than 100%?** Yes, when the part is larger than the total: 250 out of 200 is 125%.

**How is this different from a percentage of a number?** Here you know both numbers and look for the percentage. If you know the percentage and need the amount, use the [percentage calculator](/en/math/percent-of/).

**How do I find how much a value changed in percent?** Use the [percentage change calculator](/en/math/percent-change/), which compares an old and a new value.
