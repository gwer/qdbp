## How to use

Type a regular number to get its Roman numeral, or type a Roman numeral (upper or lower case) to get the number. The direction is detected automatically.

## How Roman numerals work

The symbols are I = 1, V = 5, X = 10, L = 50, C = 100, D = 500 and M = 1000. Symbols usually go from largest to smallest and are added up: XV = 15. When a smaller symbol comes before a larger one, it's subtracted: IV = 4, IX = 9, XL = 40, XC = 90, CD = 400, CM = 900.

No symbol repeats more than three times in a row, so standard notation covers the numbers from 1 to 3,999.

## Examples

- 2026 = **MMXXVI**.
- 1994 = **MCMXCIV**.
- 3999 = **MMMCMXCIX**, the largest number in standard notation.
- XIV = **14**, XL = **40**.

## FAQ

**Why isn't IIII accepted?** It's a non-standard form (you'll sometimes see it on clock faces). The converter checks for the standard form: 4 = IV.

**Is there a zero in Roman numerals?** No — Roman numerals have no zero and no negative numbers.
